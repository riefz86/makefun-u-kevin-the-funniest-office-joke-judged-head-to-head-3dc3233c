# Drafting notes — The Funniest Office Joke

## The brief, in my own words
One line about office life, under 200 characters, with a setup and a
turn, punchline last. The judge weighs, in order: a real one-line joke
about office life; a turn on something anyone who has worked in an
office recognises; surprise; every word earning its place; freshness.

## Angles I considered and rejected
- "This meeting could have been an email" — the single most obvious
  office joke. The brief itself names it as an example, so half the
  field will submit some version of it. Rejected for freshness.
- The reply-all disaster and the printer that jams on deadline day —
  both named in the brief as examples too. Same reason: rejected.
- The office fridge thief and the stolen lunch — relatable, but the
  turn is hard to land in one line without explaining the setup.
- "Per my last email" passive-aggression — funny, but it reads as a
  complaint stated plainly rather than a joke with a turn.
- Hot-desking: hunting for a chair each morning — decent, but the
  punchline ("hunting for the will to sit in it") is a sigh, not a laugh.

## The angle I kept
Asking IT for a second monitor and receiving a second meeting instead.
It is the same everyday frustration (the office answers every request
with more process), but through a door nobody else is likely to use:
hardware requests. The word "duplicates" does double duty — it is
exactly what a second monitor is, and exactly what the office did to
the calendar. That is the turn, and it is the last word group of the
line, so the punchline lands last.

## Revision
First draft said "got a second meeting instead". Revised to "they
booked me a second meeting instead": booking is an office verb, it
makes the meeting something done to the speaker, and it sharpens the
contrast with asking (a request answered by an imposition).

## Final line (137 characters)
> I asked IT for a second monitor and they booked me a second meeting instead -- apparently the only thing this office duplicates is calendar invites.
