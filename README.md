One short line about office life, built for make.fun's "The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head" bounty ($2 in USDC, judged head to head by an AI judge). The setup is an ordinary request to IT; the turn is that the office answers it with the one thing it always produces — another meeting — and the punchline lands on the word "duplicates".

The joke:

> I asked IT for a second monitor and they booked me a second meeting instead -- apparently the only thing this office duplicates is calendar invites.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the single-line joke (148 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.
